## Principles

- **Your bike progression is untouched.** Tue/Sat: 40/70, 45/80, 30/55 (recovery), 45/85, 45/100, 35/65 (recovery). Everything else is built around it.
- **One shape every week:** Mon rest · Tue bike · Wed swim + strength · Thu run · Fri rest · Sat bike · Sun run. Consistency is the phase theme, so the week does not change.
- **Hours stay in range.** Build weeks land at 3.4–4.0 h; recovery weeks at 2.9–3.2 h. Week 6 touches the ceiling because it has the 100-min ride.
- **Longest run never exceeds 35 min** (what you have done), until the knee has shown it can handle two runs a week.
- **Sunday is run-or-swim.** If the knee is above 3/10 or you are flat, swim 20–25 min easy and log that (what you did on Nov 22).

### Knee rule (every run)

0–10 scale. **≤2 and quiet by next morning:** carry on. **3–4, or still sore next morning:** shorten, add walk breaks, no progression that week. **5+, sharp, or a limp:** stop, swap in a swim or ride, and get it looked at if it lingers.

### Swim checkpoints

- **Wk 2 benchmark:** 500 yd continuous, timed. This replaces guessing.
- **Wk 5 CSS test:** 400 yd then 200 yd, both timed. Threshold pace per 100yd = (400 time − 200 time) ÷ 2. Ironman swim pace sits a few seconds per 100 slower than that.
- **Wk 7 re-test:** repeat the 500 at the same effort. Faster at the same effort is the win.

## What to log so the next adjustment is not a guess

1. **Distance on every swim** (the Nov 22 swim has a time but no distance, so no pace).
2. **Avg power and avg heart rate on rides**, if the trainer or watch gives them. With no FTP assumed, this is how we later check that "easy" really is easy.
3. **RPE on runs and a knee score (0–10) the next morning** in the notes.
4. **Strength**, even if it was only 12 minutes.

## Fueling

The Dec 19 ride (85 min) and especially the Dec 26 ride (100 min) are your first chances to practice drinking and eating on the bike. Try roughly 30–60 g of carbohydrate per hour on the 100-min ride and note how your stomach handles it. Build I leans on this; there is no reason to meet it for the first time in May.
